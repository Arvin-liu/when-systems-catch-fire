### 1. External cross-domain replay

Frozen question:

> 在城市尺度，增加树冠覆盖是否能稳定降低夏季近地面热暴露？在什么条件下这一关系不能被解释为因果？

The blinded baseline was prepared before the governed pass. The sealed replay
has `7` substantive source families plus `2` contextual/method records, with
retrieval summary `5 / 3 / 1` (directly readable / abstract or authoritative
landing-page with limits / official PDF extraction limited). The final fresh
ROLE-C review returned `PASS`.

The bounded result is that measured canopy-cover exposures were associated with
lower local summertime near-surface air temperature in the Tacoma, Portland,
and Madison settings under their observational designs. This does not identify
a universal city-scale causal planting effect. LST, near-surface air
temperature, and human heat exposure were kept separate; direct human
heat-stress exposure was `SOURCE_NOT_RECOVERED`. Canopy volume, tree presence,
shade, and generic vegetation were not substituted for canopy cover.

Source attribution is retained in the replay ledger, including [Schwaab et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-26768-w),
[Ettinger et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-51921-y),

[Meili et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ufug.2020.126970), and the [US Forest Service record](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/62536).
WMO and CDC records remain contextual/method records, not additional
substantive source families.
