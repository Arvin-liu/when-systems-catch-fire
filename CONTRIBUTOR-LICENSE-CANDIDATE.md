# Contributor License Candidate

This file is a candidate contributor-rights mechanism and is not yet effective.

A future layered or dual-license model needs clear inbound contribution terms. Candidate options include:

1. Contributor License Agreement granting the project rights to distribute contributions under the active license and future approved layered licenses.
2. Developer Certificate of Origin plus an explicit inbound=outbound rule for each marked license layer.
3. Separate contribution tracks for core software, documentation/data, schemas/interfaces, and third-party material.

Contributors must not submit material they cannot license. Third-party excerpts, generated content with uncertain rights, and imported datasets require explicit provenance notes.
