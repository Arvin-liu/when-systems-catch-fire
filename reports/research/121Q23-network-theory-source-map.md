# 121Q23 Network Theory Source Map

These sources guide representation and validator boundaries only.

| Source | Permitted Projection | Prohibited Inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rumelhart & Norman, accretion/tuning/restructuring | Distinguish surface addition, tuning and restructuring-like update modes. | Do not diagnose internal learning mechanism from one behavior. |
| Posner et al., conceptual change | New information may be rejected, gated or require accommodation conditions. | Do not claim every conceptual change follows this model. |
| Collins & Loftus, spreading activation | Represent activation/retrieval dynamics. | Retrieval, repetition or activation is not integration or truth. |
| Hopfield 1982 | Mathematical precedent for attractor-like dynamics. | No neuroscientific or psychological claim from illustrative examples. |
| Kivelä et al., multilayer networks | Layered relations and interlayer coupling. | Layer boundaries are not natural ontology. |
| Holme & Saramäki, temporal networks | Time-respecting paths and temporal edge activation. | Static aggregation must not invent time-impossible paths. |
| Battiston et al., higher-order interactions | HyperRelation and group interaction preservation. | Higher-order relations must not be silently collapsed into pairs. |
| Centola, complex contagion | Separate information exposure from behavior adoption and reinforcement. | Diffusion is not learning or durable action change. |
