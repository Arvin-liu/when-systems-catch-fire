## Validator Coverage

The validator checks probability semantics, required conditions, normalization, observation/intervention separation, declared system boundary, observed/latent separation, declared assumptions, coarse-graining map/loss/control, entropy separation, posterior boundary, high-probability overclaims, deterministic pseudo-probability, and residue.

## Claim Ceiling

PSD is a derived operational extension. It does not claim probability is the ontology of causality, does not claim system boundaries are unique, and does not unify probability theory, systems theory, and domain causality.
