## What this candidate establishes

The 12 frozen synthesis units were checked proposition-by-proposition against
independent public source families. The strongest result is bounded support for
method and measurement boundaries: define the construct, expose denominator
and timing, distinguish self-report from behavior, document evaluation and
human oversight, and keep narrative or structural analogy separate from causal
identification.

This is not a general external validation of GetNote, its source projection,
or the underlying concepts as social laws, universal mechanisms, measured
psychological facts, or causal effects.

## Source and privacy boundary

The source registry includes public papers, official frameworks, official law,
official guidance, public benchmark records, and stable DOI or publisher links.
Access-limited records are labeled as such; an abstract or search result is not
treated as full-text inspection. Normative frameworks and legal rules are
recorded as normative requirements, not proof of compliance or effectiveness.

This candidate intentionally excludes private note bodies, private note paths,
reconstructive hashes, raw GetNote exports, and excerpts that would allow a
private source to be rebuilt. Public traceability goes through the stable
source URLs and the 12-unit bounded ledger.
