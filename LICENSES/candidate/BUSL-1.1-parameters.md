# BUSL-1.1 Candidate Parameters

Candidate only. The official Business Source License 1.1 text is maintained by MariaDB and should be reviewed from the authoritative source before use.

- Licensor: project maintainer or future legal entity, pending review.
- Licensed Work: future core executable software, executors, Function OS implementations, and high-maintenance runtime tooling, as explicitly marked in a future release.
- Additional Use Grant: personal learning, education, academic research, non-commercial research, evaluation, and public-benefit nonprofit use.
- Commercial production use: requires a separate commercial license or written reciprocal agreement.
- Change Date: no later than four years after first public release of the covered version.
- Change License: AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Source-available notice: BUSL-covered material must not be described as OSI open source before the change date.
