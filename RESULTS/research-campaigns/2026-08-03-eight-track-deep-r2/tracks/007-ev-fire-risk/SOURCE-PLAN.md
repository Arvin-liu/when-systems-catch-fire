# 007 source plan

| role | required source | extraction target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Denmark primary | Beredskabsstyrelsen 2023 report and 2025 fact sheet | raw fires, stock, annual-rate definition, conventional comparator, age and revision notes |
| Sweden primary | MSB/MCF 2024 incident summary plus official vehicle-stock source | 40 vehicle-fire incidents, electric/hybrid stock and other-fuel stock, incident/reporting boundary |
| third jurisdiction cross-check | Norway DSB/SSB data where denominator is recoverable | numerator, fleet classification, whether cross-country rate is transportable |
| undercount/data quality | NIST TN 2365 | source fragmentation, confidence interval, inability to compare to ICE denominator |
| consequence boundary | RISE full-scale experiments as hosted by DEMA, plus technical experiment if needed | heat release, HF/toxic smoke, suppression, distinction from ignition probability |
| age/mileage/exposure | official fleet age/mileage tables or technical risk assessment | whether per-vehicle stock rates are confounded by fleet maturity and driving exposure |

News and fact-checks can locate these records but will not be counted as primary evidence. The Danish fact sheet’s cross-national claim is treated as a source to audit, not as three independent country studies.
