## R1 claims retained only as leads

- Denmark’s official 2025 compilation reported lower observed fires per vehicle for electric/hybrid cars than for other vehicles.
- A Swedish/Norwegian comparison pointed in the same direction, but R1 itself noted that definitions and vehicle ages differ.
- NIST’s 2026 multi-source estimate warned that EV/PHEV fire records are fragmented and may be undercounted, without providing a matched ICE denominator.
- Battery fire consequences (toxic gases, cooling and re-ignition) were distinct from ignition frequency.

## R2 repair requirements

R2 must directly read the official numerator and denominator documents, recompute at least two jurisdiction-years, inspect the apparent Denmark raw-count/rate denominator mismatch, preserve category and definition boundaries, and test whether the low observed frequency is plausibly explained by younger fleets or different exposure. R1’s broader phrase “现有证据偏向电动车起火率较低” is not promoted unless these repairs survive challenge.
