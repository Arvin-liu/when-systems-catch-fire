# 007 preregistration — denominator, definition and age audit

## Frozen research question

For battery-electric or hybrid passenger vehicles, do observed fire incidents occur more often than for combustion-vehicle comparators after the numerator, denominator, vehicle category, calendar year, vehicle age and fire definition are made explicit? Separately, do the data support any statement about consequences after ignition?

## Claim ceiling

The maximum claim is jurisdiction- and definition-limited observed fire frequency per registered/in-traffic vehicle. The study will not infer a global risk, a lifetime hazard, a per-kilometre hazard, or a causal effect of drivetrain from simple counts. A lower observed frequency will not be converted into “safe”; a special suppression/toxic-gas finding will not be converted into “more likely to ignite”.

## Required calculation

For every jurisdiction-year where inputs are available:

`rate per 10,000 = reported vehicle-fire incidents / vehicle-stock denominator × 10,000`.

The calculation must record whether the denominator is end-of-year stock, annual mean stock, vehicles in traffic, registered vehicles, sales, or an implied denominator recovered from a published rate. It must not silently mix passenger cars with vans, BEV with PHEV, or vehicle fires with battery-only fires.

## Falsifiers and downgrade rules

- If a direct same-definition official comparison reverses the Nordic direction, the scoped candidate claim is contested.
- If numerator and denominator cannot be aligned in at least two jurisdictions, terminal state is `BOUNDED_RESEARCH_BLOCKED_WITH_VERIFIABLE_EVIDENCE`.
- If a lower per-vehicle rate disappears after age or mileage adjustment, the unadjusted R1 direction is not promoted.
- If fire definitions differ materially between jurisdictions, cross-country pooling is prohibited.
- Physical severity, toxic exposure, cooling time or re-ignition is a separate outcome family and cannot adjudicate ignition probability.

## Evidence contract

Required primary evidence: official fire incidents and official stock data for at least two jurisdictions. Required independent evidence: a technical note, peer-reviewed/technical experiment, or independent data-quality analysis addressing undercounting, age, mileage, collision, charging or consequence definitions. News may locate sources but cannot prove the risk estimate.
