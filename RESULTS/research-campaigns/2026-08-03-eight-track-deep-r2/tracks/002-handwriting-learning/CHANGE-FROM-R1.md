# Change from R1

R1 was retained as a retrieval lead under `UNVERIFIED_RAPID_EVIDENCE_SCANS`. R2 read the full available PDFs and separated the claim into six outcome families.

- The R1 neural-process wording is retained only for S1's short EEG task; it is no longer a learning claim.
- The R1 “handwriting improves learning” direction is narrowed to a small, heterogeneous pooled association in some college lecture studies.
- Urry's direct replication and mini-meta are added as a counterweight against a large immediate encoding advantage.
- Mueller Study 3 and Flanigan's review moderator are retained as conditional evidence, not as a universal main effect.
- Keyboard note quantity and verbatim capture are treated as a mediator/resource whose value depends on later processing and external storage.
- Typing skill, disability/accessibility, children, non-Korean populations, and authentic courses remain open rather than being silently externalized.
