```text
S2 original experiments ── related line of work ── S3 direct replication / mini-meta
          │                                      │

               S4 2024 meta-analysis
                         │ overlaps primary studies

S1 recent EEG experiment: separate primary design, but same broad device/lecture question

```

## Audit decisions

1. S1 is counted as a separate primary experiment, but its EEG and immediate outcomes are not treated as an independent estimate of delayed achievement.
2. S2 and S3 are not counted as independent confirmations of one pooled device effect. S3 is stronger for the direct-replication question; S2 is retained for the review and factual/conceptual mechanism.
3. S4 and S6 are two synthesis methods, not two independent participant populations. Their agreement in direction is informative, while their differing magnitudes are evidence of model and inclusion sensitivity.
4. S5 is not used to quantify a skill interaction because the accessible report does not expose the required denominator and effect details.
5. R1 is a candidate-claim source only and is never added as scientific evidence.

## Consequence for certainty

The number of URLs is not the number of independent tests. The final confidence is driven by convergence across distinct outcome families and by the fact that direct replication, original experiments, and syntheses do not produce one identical estimate. The proper label is `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_COMPETING_ESTIMANDS`, with external adjudication still required.
