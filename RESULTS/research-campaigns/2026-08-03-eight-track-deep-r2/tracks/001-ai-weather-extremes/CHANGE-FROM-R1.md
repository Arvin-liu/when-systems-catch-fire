# Change from R1

R1 verdict: `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`.

R2 preserves the primary direction but narrows the identity and claim ceiling. The paper and supplement were read in full; the training/test years, grid, record definition, event duplication, metrics, truth variants and limitations were located. An independent peer-reviewed percentile-tail benchmark and an independent three-event study were added. FastNet was separated into physical-guided proof-of-principle rather than treated as a replacement result. A public coarse-grid WeatherBench2 reconstruction was completed and explicitly marked as non-equivalent to the paper headline.

The promoted R2 wording is not “HRES wins extreme weather.” It is: “HRES wins the specified Zhang record-exceedance benchmark; cross-benchmark evidence is context-dependent, and operational replacement is unsupported.”
