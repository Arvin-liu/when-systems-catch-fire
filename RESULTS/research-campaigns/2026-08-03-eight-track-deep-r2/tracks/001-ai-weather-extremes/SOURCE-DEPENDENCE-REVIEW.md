# 001 source-dependence review

The evidence is not eight independent confirmations. Zhang et al. are the承重 primary. WeatherBench2 supplies a shared public data/protocol surface and is used for a coarse reanalysis, not counted as a second outcome paper. Pasche’s repository supports its own paper’s case-study reproducibility and is not a separate sample. FastNet’s technical papers and the Met Office release share an institutional project; only the technical paper is counted, and its result is treated as method proof-of-principle. ECMWF is a review and its cited studies are not re-counted. RealBench and NWP-Benchmark are methodological challenges without validated outcomes.

Olivetti & Messori is the main independent competing outcome source. Its author set, common grid, percentile definition, and 1.5° evaluation differ from Zhang’s record benchmark, even though both evaluate related model families. Pasche is independent at the case-study level but not a broad population estimate. The two sources therefore establish that the benchmark outcome is not transportable, not that one side has a larger independent evidence count.

The final report must preserve the following hierarchy: primary paper result > independent competing benchmark > public coarse reproduction > methodological warnings. News and institutional releases cannot move a claim upward in this hierarchy.
