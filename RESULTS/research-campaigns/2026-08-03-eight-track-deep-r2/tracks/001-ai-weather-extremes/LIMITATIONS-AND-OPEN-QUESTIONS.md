# 001 limitations and open questions

1. The public reanalysis/forecast reconstruction is 64×32 and therefore cannot validate the Zhang 0.25° headline table or every model checkpoint.
2. Record gridpoints are spatially dependent; an event-level clustering and uncertainty analysis is still needed.
3. ERA5, station observations and operational analyses can disagree on local extremes; a station-based truth audit is outstanding.
4. The primary comparison does not identify the causal contribution of architecture, training distribution, resolution, initialization, post-processing or physical constraints.
5. A harmonized benchmark should hold truth, initial conditions, variables, resolution, model version, lead and spatial aggregation constant, then report both record and quantile estimands.
6. Future operational claims require prospective station verification, probabilistic calibration, warning-decision thresholds, false-alarm/missed-event costs and independent governance.
