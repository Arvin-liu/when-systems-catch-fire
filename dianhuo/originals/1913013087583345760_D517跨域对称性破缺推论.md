English: Physical unification pursues gate surface reduction (D183 gate surface merger unification function), social evolution pursues gate surface increase (Tang-Song 1 gate surface → Ming 2 gate surfaces → modern multi-gate surfaces), the two directions are opposite, but both are Φ = zero-temperature free energy optimization at the bottom.
