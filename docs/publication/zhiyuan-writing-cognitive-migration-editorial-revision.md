# 认知迁移编辑修订模块（当前 main 重建候选）

English: **Cognitive-Migration Editorial Revision**

Candidate version: `0.1.0-candidate`
Status: `REBUILT_CANDIDATE_NOT_INTEGRATED`
Operational location: L6 editorial revision after a stable draft

This candidate is rebuilt from current main `e5c6d1d0b75dae41b414474bc22747816cd00c78`.
It does not modify the current `之元写作法 0.5.0`, its historical versions, or
the two source works used for the examples. It is not PR #189 replayed or
cherry-picked.

## Scope

The module answers one bounded question:

> Once a draft's factual boundary, claim ceiling, and conceptual movement are
> stable, how can an editor make the reader's change of understanding visible
> without announcing every turn as a formula?

It is an optional post-generation editorial pass. It may be declined by a
long-form essay, a literary piece, or an academic explanation when the pass
would flatten the work.

The module can reorganize order, emphasis, concrete anchors, and transitions.
It cannot create evidence, repair an unsupported claim, adjudicate a source,
authorize a task, run a research queue, or infer that reader response proves a
method or a fact.

## Activation gate

Before editing, record:

1. the draft commit or immutable source locator;
2. the current factual boundary and claim ceiling;
3. the intended reader movement in one sentence;
4. the people, events, differences, and counterevidence that must survive;
5. the passages whose evidence or uncertainty must not be compressed away.

If any of these are unstable, the result is `DO_NOT_ACTIVATE_EDITORIAL_PASS`.
The module is not a substitute for research, source verification, or the
Language–Thought Logic Plane.

## Editorial procedure

### 1. Freeze the movement, not a slogan

Write the starting understanding and the revised understanding separately.
They must describe a change in what the reader can distinguish, not merely a
topic or a stronger adjective. Keep the statement backstage; it is an
editing control, not an obligatory final sentence.

### 2. Diagnose the obstruction

Use a non-exclusive diagnosis:

- a concept is dense but not grounded;
- a chronology is accurate but hides the decisive turn;
- parallel examples have no governing question;
- a contrast is doing all the argumentative work on the surface;
- the draft is already coherent and needs only local repair.

Diagnosis selects editorial pressure. It is not a genre taxonomy and does not
prescribe a paragraph count.

### 3. Choose a structural carrier

Possible carriers include recursive questioning, progressive deepening,
perspective switching, selective concrete anchoring, or a return that gives an
earlier detail a new but bounded meaning. Combine them only where the source
draft supports the movement. Do not rotate mechanisms mechanically.

### 4. Rebuild locally and preserve the floor

For each changed passage, retain a recoverable before version and record:

- what changed in order, focalization, or pressure;
- which source-backed fact or uncertainty anchors the change;
- which lower-level person, event, or counterexample remains visible;
- whether any claim, causal verb, certainty, or scope changed.

When a claim changes, stop the editorial pass and route it back to the proper
research or governance process. A smooth sentence is not permission to cross a
claim ceiling.

### 5. Remove exposed scaffolding without banning precise language

Repeated `不是……而是……`, `你以为……其实……`, serial rhetorical questions,
or identical "deepening" phrases may signal that the movement is being
announced rather than carried. Replace them only when the structure can carry
the distinction. A necessary negative or contrast that protects a boundary
must remain.

### 6. Run the final audit

Check factual fidelity, evidence links, causal language, counterevidence,
affected people, sentence breath, reader recoverability, and whether the text
has become a style imitation. Reject any "golden sentence" that increases
shareability by deleting uncertainty or flattening competing explanations.

## Relation to the current method

The current method remains `0.5.0` and remains the generative/publication
method. This candidate is a separate editorial action after generation:

`stable draft → optional editorial revision → publication review`

It does not add a level, replace the Language–Thought Logic Plane, or alter the
method's source and feedback contracts. It also has no dependency on Task115,
Research Executive OS, agent-agnostic runtime, MCF, PSD, or ARN. Those systems
cannot be used as a literary quality score or as a truth upgrade.
