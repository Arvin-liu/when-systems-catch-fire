# Vehicle Ownership Fabric

Machine file: `vehicle-ownership.json`

This is a local social-technical example, not the center of 121Q21R.

It represents purchase, ownership persistence, repeated use, sale/retirement, and residual tails. The important result is that a vehicle purchase is not a single edge. It activates a state whose benefits and burdens branch across owner convenience, parking, maintenance, insurance, road risk, energy use, emissions, waste, and other affected subjects.

Boundary: this example does not prove that any specific owner creates any specific harm. It only shows what must be represented before such a claim could be responsibly tested.

Key sources used as provenance include IPCC AR6 WGIII Chapter 10 transport and EPA greenhouse-gas emission source summaries.
