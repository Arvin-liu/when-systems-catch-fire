# Stellar Nucleosynthesis Fabric

Machine file: `stellar-nucleosynthesis.json`

This example represents a physical-scale chain: stellar fusion/nucleosynthesis, material dispersal, enriched medium, radiation or signatures reaching an observer, and observation limits.

The important result is that cosmic-scale representation needs horizons, observer frame, model dependence, and unmapped residue. A future light cone is not a complete causal mechanism, and an observation is not the whole universe graph.

Boundary: this is not a complete cosmological network and does not claim to unify stellar physics, thermodynamics, and life.

Key source used as provenance: NASA Science, `Are we really made of star stuff?`
