# Task185 Step05 — Minimal Cognitive Method Kernel Candidate R0

**Candidate:** `MINIMAL_COGNITIVE_METHOD_KERNEL_CANDIDATE_R0`

**Status:** research-only, noncanonical; not a final definition.

This candidate is derived only from the frozen source universe and Steps01–04. It distinguishes implementation phenotype, observable method records and metacognitive transition records. It describes what could be inspected and falsified; it does not claim that an agent has these abilities or that transfer succeeded.

## 1. Replaceable representation / phenotype

Models/providers, prompts, schemas, registries, tools/validators, workflows/runtimes, routing overlays and Git packages are carriers. They can realize, record or test a method, but the carrier is not the method and its presence is not evidence of capability. Task179 makes this distinction explicit in `CURRENT_SELF_MODEL_R0`; prior runtime, Foundation and Knowledge mechanisms already existed as separate surfaces.

## 2. Candidate inheritable method cycle

| Method slot | Existing evidence | Task179–181 change | Gap / ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame the question and identify objects | Foundation identities, task boundaries and claim objects. | R0 adds a separate versioned cognitive object/relation representation. | A uniform task-to-method framing behavior is not measured. |
| Retrieve by provenance and bind evidence | Knowledge Experience, Task172 exact-ID routing, Foundation evidence/claim binding. | Task179 reuses routing and provenance-bound fixtures. | No shared asset/relation → method-use → transfer chain. |
| Separate observation, evidence, analogy and hypothesis | Foundation claim gates, mechanism adjudication and source-specific boundaries. | R0 enumerates this as an observable method slot. | Correct behavior on unseen tasks is unmeasured. |
| Preserve uncertainty and expose boundaries | Existing pending/abstain/ceiling practices; ESI and Foundation boundaries. | Cognitive IR/transition schemas type unknowns and unknown relations. | Mechanical preservation is not reliable uncertainty recognition. |
| Consider alternatives and counterexamples | Foundation counterexample gate, M0/M1 mechanism alternatives, Q37/Q38 research. | R0 records alternatives, boundaries and evaluation refs. | Alternative coverage and correct use are not established. |
| Choose an action or next test within a bounded loss | 121Q16 records alternatives, affordable loss, rollback and non-claims; runtime has action budgets. | R0 makes action selection a method slot and has a schema-family complexity budget. | General selection skill and stakes/cause-scale proportionality are unmeasured. |
| Detect failure/anomaly and retain provenance | Failure typology, Q39 pilot, self-correction and runtime operational memory. | R0 transition accepts anomaly/failure refs. | The stores are separate; memory/remediation does not prove method transfer. |
| Revise method and retain migration lineage | Repository correction, task history and adapters exist. | R0 introduces one explicit cognitive transition contract with disposition and unknowns. | No independent evidence that the revision improves behavior or generalizes. |
| Independently test transfer without promotion | Mechanism-adjudication and stance-blind review precede R0. | Task179 freezes a blind-first package; Task181 isolates evaluation evidence. | Metrics are not measured; this task ran no evaluator or successor. |

The minimum cycle is therefore observable and evidence-bounded: identify/frame → retrieve with provenance → bind evidence and uncertainty → expose boundaries/alternatives → choose a bounded next action/test → record observation/failure → revise only with lineage → keep independent evaluation and authority external. Each arrow is a candidate contract; none is a proven cognitive ability.

## 3. Candidate metacognitive evolution cycle

1. **Notice possible inadequacy.** Tie an anomaly or unresolved question to a stated limitation of the old representation; keep observation apart from interpretation.
2. **Propose a reframing.** Preserve old and new representations/methods, expected discriminating observations, known alternatives and likely information/complexity gains and losses.
3. **Compare without self-verdict.** Use a frozen baseline, explicit fixtures and independent criteria; keep schema conformance and CI distinct from continuation or transfer results.
4. **Record disposition.** Use `ADOPT`, `REJECT`, `COEXIST` or `UNRESOLVED`; preserve source refs, unknowns, migration, rollback and retirement conditions.

Task179 supplies a typed transition/migration contract and a candidate complexity budget. The repository does not yet show measured comparative gain/loss, general method-use lineage, or independent transfer. The cycle is thus partly formalized, not demonstrated end-to-end.

## Explicit non-claims and stop boundaries

- This is not a final definition of cognition, an ontology, or a replacement for Foundation/Current.
- No hidden chain-of-thought or private reasoning trace is requested or stored.
- A schema, tag, registry, retrieval hit, test, CI result or successful workflow does not establish cognition or inheritance.
- Existing unknowns and alternative explanations stay visible; no package-level conclusion can authorize promotion.
- No R1, MetaRSI, Model-RSI, weight training, Successor or Evaluator is authorized by this candidate.

**Candidate falsifier:** If blind, source-bound reconstruction cannot recover the method and its limits—or if an independent control cannot distinguish transfer from schema/style imitation—downgrade this to an artifact taxonomy, split the affected slots, or leave the result unresolved. Any field removal or migration still needs its own authorized, reversible contract.
