## Synthesis

Task179–181 did not invent identity, evidence, provenance, claim ceilings, retrieval, routing, failure records, rollback, task handoff, blind review, or authority separation. Those mechanisms existed in narrower owners and were often already mechanically guarded. The material increment is their composition around an explicit cognitive object and transition contract, plus a provider-neutral capability envelope and a separate evidence-admission boundary.

The term “cognitive inheritance” still outruns the repository proof. The exact new contracts make a candidate transfer inspectable and falsifiable; they do not establish that a method was learned, preserved, reconstructed, improved, or transferred between models. The most defensible novelty statement is therefore: *new repository-local formalization and composition, with transfer capability unresolved*.
