## The umbrella versus marriage/job/migration question

**No general mechanism is evidenced in the inspected scope that distinguishes the cognitive investment appropriate for carrying an umbrella from that for marriage, employment, or migration.** The repository has relevant ingredients, not the allocator: a case-specific decision record compares three bounded project actions and defines affordable loss/rollback; causal architecture has reachability and propagation fields; Task179’s complexity budget limits implementation surface, not deliberation. No reusable mapping from stakes, downstream reach, duration, irreversibility, uncertainty, and option lock-in to additional evidence, consultation, time, or verification was found.

Thus the scoped answer is “not demonstrated,” rather than “proved absent everywhere.” The 30-day window belongs to external-signal observation, not to the duration or cognitive weight of a life decision.

## Evidence interpretation

- `affordable_loss` bounds a particular pilot; it is not a universal risk ceiling.
- `claim_ceiling` bounds what evidence supports; it is not the maximum harm a decision may cause.
- `CausalConeOrHorizon` represents a reachability boundary; it is not causal proof and not a decision allocator.
- `complexity-budget-r0.json` controls schema/runtime/persistent-authority surface; it does not set model or human reasoning time.
- `framing_deltas` tracks declared language-transform changes; it does not by itself measure AI influence or decision salience.
