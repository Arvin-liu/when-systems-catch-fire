Its own crosswalk identifies several of these as gaps rather than pretending the substrate was absent in every form.

4. The most visible missing link across the older lines is not raw storage alone: the source set does not show one inspectable chain from an asset/relation, through method-use and a recorded method change, to independently assessed transfer and controlled retirement. Step02 onward tests this as a candidate gap; this Step01 timeline does not promote it into a canonical claim.
