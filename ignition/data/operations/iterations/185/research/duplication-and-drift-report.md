# Task185 Step04 — Duplication, Fork and Semantic-Drift Audit

## Method

Pairs were compared by input, output, authority, consumer and validation boundary—not by shared names. Maintenance cost, cognitive load, migration risk and deletion risk are qualitative, per-finding estimates; they are not additive scores. The scoped comparison covers the Step01 timeline, Step02 mechanism atlas and source-backed Task179–181 contracts. It does not claim a complete grep of every historical implementation or an absence proof across the full archive.

## Findings

1. **No exact duplicate was established.** The strongest repeated fields are provenance, unknowns and claim boundaries across the R0 self-model, Cognitive IR, transition and capability packages. Their object grains and consumers differ; that is a real maintenance/ontology-pressure signal, not sufficient evidence to merge them.
2. **Retrieval has functional overlap.** Knowledge Experience offers broad generated navigation/search; Task172 routing adds facets, bounded fallback and exact-ID validation. Both can return assets, but one does not replace the other.
3. **Authority-bearing registries and Cognitive IR are a false similarity.** They share object-shaped fields while operating at different authority levels. Treating their rows as interchangeable would risk promotion or identity collision.
4. **Failure memory, runtime operational memory and self-correction overlap but remain separate.** One retains failure/recurrence/rollback context, another projects repository changes into claim/evidence impact and remediation. Their retention, sensitivity and authority differ.
5. **Self-correction is not cognitive transition.** A generated repository repair/audit does not show that an agent reframed a representation or adopted a method. A transition record is also not evidence that the correction worked.
6. **Task179 evaluation design and Task181 evaluation-evidence isolation are complementary.** One freezes the evaluation contract; the other constrains where produced evidence may flow. Removing either creates a different class of gap.
7. **Adjacent validators are not one duplicate gate.** Repository path accounting, Cognitive IR/package schema validation and Task181 admission exclusions cover different universes. Their edges merit cross-layer contract tests, not consolidation into a broad validator with ambiguous authority.
8. **Capability is an overloaded term.** Runtime permissions/executor operations and R0 cognitive-interface contracts are separate namespaces; the R0 calibration state itself says most operations are not measured.
9. **Decision-integrity, mechanism adjudication and the 121Q16 affordable-loss record overlap around choices but ask distinct questions:** whether the process was frozen fairly, which causal paths fit evidence, and which bounded action was authorized. None is a general cognitive-effort allocator.
10. **Observation has semantic drift across domains.** External-interface exposure, local process observation, and probabilistic partial observation are not one world-feedback sensor. Task140 explicitly limits its plane to local repository observation/reconciliation.
11. **Analogy discovery and canonical isomorphism gates are complementary.** Archived Q37/Q38 artifacts can generate mappings/residue; Foundation constrains claim strength. No direct adoption or behavior-equivalence proof was found.
12. **A superseded exhaustive architecture did not reappear as such in R0.** The current governance mapping labels the old 2026-07-09 meta-protocol→function→case hierarchy superseded/historical; the Task179 substrate has a narrower, provisional consumer and does not restore it. No exact abandoned-path reactivation was found in the inspected Task179–181 line.

## Risk disposition

- Highest deletion risk attaches to canonical authority registries, source/evaluation provenance, historical negative evidence and rollback/repair lineage; preserve them unless a separately authorized migration proves a lossless replacement.
- Highest migration risk attaches to merging Foundation claims with Cognitive IR, external and local observations, or capability permissions with cognitive capability metadata.
- Highest current cognitive-load pressure is the repeated “object / relation / evidence / uncertainty / boundary / transition” vocabulary across R0 package layers. The minimal next evidence is consumer-level field tracing and a reconstruction test, not a schema merge by visual similarity.
- Maintenance costs can be reduced by explicit typed references and contract tests at real handoffs. Bulk unification would add more authority ambiguity than it removes.

**Step04 status:** no deletion, migration, registry consolidation or canonical change performed. A few overlapping R0 fields remain `NEEDS_ADJUDICATION`; the audit recommends further evidence, not a repair.
