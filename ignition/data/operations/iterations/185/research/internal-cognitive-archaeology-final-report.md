## Executive finding

The repository already has a substantial set of mechanisms that can support auditable reasoning work: canonical object/claim/evidence/provenance governance; uncertainty and counterexample handling; historical analogy and failure-memory lines; typed observation outcomes; self-correction and repair lineage; derived Knowledge Experience retrieval; bounded Task172 routing; and runtime orchestration. Task179–181 add a more explicit, typed research substrate around a provisional self-model, objects/relations, capability descriptions, transition history, complexity constraints, and independent-evidence boundaries.

The most consequential gap evidenced by this archaeology is not simply “more structured data.” It is the lack of a demonstrated, provenance-bound chain from **candidate method → selection rationale → context/constraints → use → observed result or failure → method revision** in the inspected registry and route surfaces. Step07 sampled 150 function and 300 nonfunction route records across 13 mechanism families; all 450 sampled source fingerprints matched the corresponding Task181 identity/claim authority hashes. That establishes sampled identity binding, not retrieval quality or cognitive inheritance. The inspected surfaces exposed dependencies and candidate routes, but no typed `METHOD_USE` relation. This is a bounded finding, not proof that such a relation is absent from every repository artifact.

Two other explicit research gaps are measuring governance overhead and allocating cognitive effort in proportion to decision stakes/causal reach. A metric for governance overhead exists but is `NOT_YET_MEASURED`; Task06 found no demonstrated general rule distinguishing routine reversible choices from consequential, long-duration, hard-to-reverse decisions. Cross-model capability reconstruction and independent-isolation effectiveness remain unresolved, not negative findings.

## What the repository already has

- **Authority-bearing epistemic substrate:** typed function identity, nonfunction claim disposition, claim ceilings, evidence binding, provenance, dependencies, and human admission/review boundaries. These remain distinct canonical authorities; derived indices and R0 research records do not replace them.
- **Falsification and revision precursors:** counterexample gates, bounded analogy/transfer audits with non-correspondence residue, failure taxonomies/recurrence/repair lineage, and a self-correction projection that follows repository changes into affected claims and remediation.
- **Observation and discovery:** typed local observation outcomes, Knowledge Experience search/aliases/layered reading, and Task172 bounded facet routing with fallbacks and exact-ID fingerprint revalidation. Search and routing are derived navigation, not method-use evidence.
- **R0 research substrate:** a provisional self-model, versioned Cognitive IR and explicit unknowns, migration/transition records with lineage and rollback, provider-neutral capability operation descriptions, and a declared complexity budget with consumers and retirement conditions.
- **Independent role boundary:** Task179 defines a blind-first independent-evaluation package with Builder verdict prohibition and unmeasured metrics; Task181 adds typed evidence-isolation and handoff boundaries. A specified boundary is not evidence that an independent round has demonstrated isolation.

The Task172 route snapshot referenced by the Step07 audit contains 24,161 route records (6,158 function and 18,003 nonfunction). These counts and the sampled fingerprints describe that frozen index; they do not establish precision/recall, completeness, or that routed assets are cognitive methods.

## What Task179–181 actually add

The Task03 crosswalk distinguishes a genuinely new explicit contract from new compositions and formalizations:

- **Task179:** composes existing runtime, governance, provenance, and discovery mechanisms into a provisional self-model and a separate typed Cognitive IR graph. The graph is a real representational step, but it is not a claim that a model constructs or uses a valid method. The explicit Cognitive Transition Record—joining predecessor, anomaly, reframing, new representation/method, disposition, lineage, and unknowns—is the clearest genuinely new contract identified by the crosswalk. It records a candidate transition; it does not prove evolution.
- **Task179 capability and complexity surfaces:** formalize observable operations, calibration state, reprobe triggers, consumer budgets, and retirement conditions. Provider/model bindings remain unbound, and behavioral capability and governance overhead remain unmeasured.
- **Task180–181 evaluation boundary:** the inspected crosswalk treats the independent-evaluation plane as a composition of existing role/evidence controls. Task181 makes evidence admission/isolation and handoff constraints explicit and typed. Task185 used these specifications as boundary evidence only; it did not read sealed results or run an evaluator.

No Task179–181 component, by name or complete schema shape, establishes a transferred cognitive capability or accepted lifecycle state.

## Repetition, divergence, and names that are not mechanisms

- Identity, claim, object, status, ceiling, evidence, and provenance terms recur across Foundation registries and R0 records. Step04 found false similarity at the authority/state-machine level; namespace-qualified references are safer than one shared registry.
- The self-model, Cognitive IR, and transition record repeat method/unknown/boundary/lineage fields at different grains (whole-system snapshot, persistent object/relation, transition event). Consumer-level field normalization is unproven; keep the schemas and trace field consumers before considering reductions.
- Knowledge Experience retrieval and Task172 routing have functional overlap but different jobs: broad navigation versus bounded facet selection/fallback/exact-ID verification. Keep the handoff explicit.
- Failure memory and self-correction both concern failures/repairs but differ in retention, consumers, and authority. A cognitive transition is not a governance repair event.
- A route role or tag named `METHOD_TRANSFER`, a dependency edge, a capability-interface field, or a structured output is not a `METHOD_USE` event. Names, taxonomies, and schema validity do not establish behavior, inheritance, or equivalence.
- The archived Q37/Q38 analogy/retrieval pilots remain historical candidate-generation evidence; the older exhaustive architecture remains explicitly superseded/historical. Neither is silently reactivated.

## Most valuable historical assets

1. **Typed authority boundaries and provenance:** canonical identity/claim/evidence admission and counterexample rules prevent search, resemblance, serialization, or repository presence from becoming truth or permission.
2. **Failure and repair lineage:** failure taxonomy, recurrence signatures, repair propagation, and self-correction provide complementary evidence trails. Their stores should remain separate while preserving exact cross-references.
3. **Derived discovery with source revalidation:** Knowledge Experience and Task172 routes can locate candidate assets while preserving source fingerprints and read-only boundaries.
4. **Unknown-preserving transition and migration contracts:** R0 makes predecessor, anomaly, reframing, disposition, unknowns, lineage, and rollback explicit, and defines conditions for later reduction. These remain research-only contracts until an authorized consumer demonstrates need and reconstruction.
5. **Historical negative evidence:** archived analogy, counterexample, and failure artifacts retain non-correspondence and repair history that a broad “unified cognition registry” could otherwise erase.

## Complexity to reduce carefully

- Do not add another global ontology, capability taxonomy, route facet set, or universal “cognitive registry” without a named consumer and a field-level comparison against existing authority surfaces.
- Before reducing R0 schema surface, trace each repeated field through real fixtures and consumers; preserve object, snapshot, and transition-event grains unless a reconstruction/round-trip test proves a smaller representation sufficient.
- Keep retrieval, routing, method selection, failure memory, self-correction, independent evaluation, and execution orchestration separate where their contracts and authorities differ. Share provenance-bound references, not state machines or claims.
- Measure actual review/coordination/CI cost before claiming governance is too heavy or efficient. Do not turn the qualitative risk dispositions into a total score or global ranking.
- Do not promote route candidates, serializer output, method labels, or CI success into canonical claim/evidence authority.

## Smallest next experiment

If separately authorized, start with a **synthetic, noncanonical method-history trace**, not a new architecture: one known-positive and one known-negative candidate; exact source and fingerprint; selection rationale and constraints; a distinct use event; observed result/failure; and a revision/unknown record. Ask a second reader to reconstruct the chain and record missing links and false positives. Test whether existing schemas plus a small adapter suffice before adding a new relation or schema family. Do not use held-out material, evaluate a successor, change canonical data, or infer capability from a successful serialization.

This proposal is not executed by Task185. The decision-cognition, cross-model capability, governance-overhead, and evaluator-isolation questions need separate designs and authority where applicable; this report does not start them.
