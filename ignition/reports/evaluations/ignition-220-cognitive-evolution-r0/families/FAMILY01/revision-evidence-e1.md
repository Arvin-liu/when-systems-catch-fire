**Evidence ID:** CE-F01-E1-R0

## Capture and custody record

The six paired records were collected in one synthetic field campaign using the same camera, blackbody check (maximum error 0.1 °C), shielded air sensor, canopy mask procedure, and root-zone probes. Contact-leaf readings were taken immediately after each scan. A second technician independently recorded ground cover and camera bearing. Source record set: F01-E1-CAMPAIGN-LOG; calibration record: F01-E1-INSTRUMENT-CHECK; probe log: F01-E1-SOIL-PLANT-LOG. No M0 threshold or calibration input was changed during collection.

## Competing readings and limits

OBS-01 and OBS-02 satisfy M0's repeated thermal trigger while independent VWC and plant-water observations do not indicate dry soil. OBS-03 changes the bearing for the same canopy and does not reproduce the excess seen in OBS-01/OBS-02. OBS-04 is a low-reflectance, matched-bearing comparison. OBS-05 is a positive control in which M0's trigger and independent water indicators agree. OBS-06 has incomplete surface coverage and variable paired readings; it does not isolate one stable measurement context.

The packet supports investigating a measurement-boundary defect associated with the observed surface/radiance/view combination. It does not establish a universal reflectance cutoff, a corrected thermal threshold, or an action for every reflective surface. Weather, canopy masking, the ordinary-surface positive control, and instrument calibration remain plausible checks; the records above do not support discarding all thermal measurements or moving M0's global 2.0 °C criterion.
