## Competing readings and limits

The named composition measurements co-vary with distinct turbidity-to-mass outcomes in this packet, while the instrument and gravimetric controls remain acceptable. The records are sufficient to show that applying one M0 estimate to all sampled compositions can exceed its original uncertainty band. The intermediate and aliquot-variable samples do not establish a universal composition cutoff or resolve how every mixture should be treated.

A tempting response would be to average all observed groups into a new single line, or to discard turbidity as a measurement for every sediment. Neither follows from these records: the mineral-dominant rows continue to match the M0 calibration, while composition-uncertain rows have no reproducible basis for a point mapping here.
