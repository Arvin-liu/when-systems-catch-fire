schema. It does not create an R0.5 protocol, a second method-use schema, a new
case schema, or a new condition schema. The three fresh cases and their
FACTS_ONLY, METHOD_TRACE, and BROKEN_METHOD_TRACE_CONTROL packets are data and

local-only execution boundaries, operational separation, and no-promotion

Task198 does not claim to be R0.4 or reuse it as a second method-use schema.

replication trial. It remains `NOT_RUN` here. Any later interpretation is
limited by the sealed Step05 replication logic and Step06 evaluator package:
repeated association can remain a bounded method-use signal, but it cannot by
itself establish a causal METHOD_TRACE effect, general cognitive inheritance,
R1, cross-model transfer, or canonical promotion.
