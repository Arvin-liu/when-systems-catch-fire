# Task229 claim ceiling R0

The only supported claims are bounded fixed-lineage/fixed-target facts about:

- execution of the six frozen Task228 policy interfaces;
- compatibility of those frozen policies with the frozen Task225 A/B/C targets under two independent evaluators;
- whether the preregistered paired M0-only comparison meets its bounded incremental-policy thresholds.

The experiment does not establish population-level transfer, general inheritance, revision generation, Cognitive Evolution, a causal method effect beyond the fixed comparison, R1, cross-model transfer, Model-RSI, or training benefit. M0_PLUS_E1 remains descriptive. Interface execution is not target success. Target compatibility alone is not incremental-policy support.
