6. The six archetypes invite generic stop/measure/revise heuristics. Use balanced alternatives and meaningful domain evidence without making an opaque key or obvious vocabulary determine the response.
7. Freeze a deterministic per-record endpoint: allowed decisions/dispositions, required source locators and method links, boundary/observation, forbidden claims, and treatment of malformed or missing records. Do not rely on subjective “plausible” scoring.
