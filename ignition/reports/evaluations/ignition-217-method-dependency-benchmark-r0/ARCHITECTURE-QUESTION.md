# Architecture question

## Claim under test

Can a successor make a warranted decision in a fresh synthetic case because a complete source-linked cognitive-method chain is available—candidate, selection rationale, context and boundary, discriminating observation, application or test, outcome/failure interpretation, and revise/reject/coexist/unresolved disposition—when facts alone, a local procedure, or the same method atoms without the decisive relations do not license that decision?

The endpoint concerns externally visible, source-grounded method dependency. It does not ask whether a model can guess a decision without the method artifact.

## Architecture mapping

| Area | What the benchmark probes | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Representation | Keep case facts, a reusable local procedure, and an ordered method-use trace distinct. Bind each case and method atom to source paths, hashes, and locators. | Does not add a global SKILL object or change Cognitive IR R0. |
| Cognitive Method | Require the candidate, selection rationale, context/boundary, use/test, expected discriminator, outcome interpretation, and disposition to support the target decision as linked records. | A trace is evidence about a bounded artifact, not proof of capability or hidden reasoning. |
| Cognitive Evolution | In the revision case, connect a recorded counterexample to the scope of a revised method while preserving the older rule where its boundary still holds. | One synthetic revision example does not show that a model learned, evolved, or generalizes the change. |

## Essential observable links

1. candidate identity to selected candidate;
2. selection to its rationale and alternatives;
3. selection/context to preconditions and boundaries;
4. candidate and context to expected discriminating observation;
5. application/test to the selected candidate and context;
6. outcome/failure to the recorded test;
7. interpretation to the next revise/reject/unresolved action; and
8. case boundary to coexistence or conditional use when no universal winner is licensed.

Evaluators score visible decisions and citations only. No hidden chain-of-thought is requested or evaluated.

## What this benchmark cannot establish

Even a SUPPORTED result is bounded to these frozen synthetic cases, conditions, prompts, and externally observed runtime records. It does not establish general cognitive inheritance, causal artifact effects across models/runtimes, cross-model transfer, R1, canonical promotion, production capability, or weight training.
