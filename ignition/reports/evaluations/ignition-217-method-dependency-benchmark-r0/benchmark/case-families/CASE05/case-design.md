# CASE05 design note

Family: revision lineage with a bounded counterexample and a still-valid older scope.

Freshness: a synthetic access-controlled archive policy, not a measurement or device-state case.

Decision-bearing relation: link the recorded mixed-class counterexample to the revised method's narrower scope, then apply that scope to the present mixed set without declaring universal supersession.

Facts/skill insufficiency review: the two bundle actions are operationally plausible. The facts do not supply the historical counterexample, revision link, or scope of the older rule. A packaging skill can preserve labels but cannot select a method version or interpret the lineage.

Leakage guard: no method version is present in the successor-visible facts. The method record carries provenance and version scope; it does not record this bundle as already produced.
