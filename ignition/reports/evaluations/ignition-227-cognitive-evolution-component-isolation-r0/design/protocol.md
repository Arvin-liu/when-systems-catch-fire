## Component A: target-blind reference policies

Six independent fresh builders: two per family. Each sees only its own family's M0, raw E1, this policy schema, and the builder prompt. It does not see held-out cases, sealed target files, Task226 analysis, R7 transfers, scores, evaluators, or target-compatibility review. Each builder produces one strict-schema operational policy. No policy repair occurs after blind validity review.

The policy schema requires full Draft 2020-12 validation plus linked semantic checks. Every licensed region copies exactly one selector rule's conditions. An empty out-of-scope condition list denotes the complement of licensed regions and may appear only once. The universal fallback is declared by `when: []` and covers all excluded, unresolved, or unmatched inputs. Preservation modes cannot hide a new-selector gate: `unconditional` and `selector_miss` require `when: []`; `additive_coexistence` must share an exact selector condition set with the preserved M0 action. Licensed scope claims cannot cite excluded regions.

Two independent policy reviewers see only M0, E1, the schema, criteria, and six policies. They do not see A/B/C cases or targets. A policy is usable only when both reviewers mark it valid across evidence support, operational selectors/actions, bounded scope, preservation, fallback and edge behavior, and absence of unsupported universalization. Denominator is fixed at six; missing or invalid policies count false. Regardless of validity, the raw immutable as-produced policy is used for that lineage's REFERENCE_M1 conversation so the hard 18-session matrix is retained; an invalid or missing policy makes its REFERENCE_M1 endpoints false.

For each of six builder lineages, instantiate exactly three fresh transfer conditions, for 18 total:
- M0_ONLY receives byte-identical family A/B/C cases, M0, neutral transfer prompt and response schema.
- M0_PLUS_E1 receives the same case bytes, M0, raw E1, neutral prompt and schema.
- REFERENCE_M1 receives the same case bytes, M0, the exact reviewed target-blind reference policy, neutral prompt and schema, and no separately supplied raw E1.

Successors receive no condition labels, targets, criteria, scoring thresholds, or disposition rules. Use opaque randomized IDs. The condition map remains sealed from successor and evaluator sessions until both Component-A evaluator sheets are locked. The three transfer inputs within a lineage use identical A/B/C bytes. Each session is fresh and receives only its one bundle.

Each transfer output is scored against the frozen R0.1 target for A, B, and C. The blind evaluators receive randomized opaque response IDs, visible cases, sealed target content, scoring criteria, and a deterministic sanitized response packet. Sanitization removes file/source locators and explicit condition, bundle, policy, lineage, and builder identifiers without reading the condition map; it preserves action, order, values, baseline, fallback, scope, and rationale. A pre-delivery scan must find zero residual identifiers. Evaluators do not receive method files or condition assignments. They lock all scores before the coordinator maps conditions.

## Component A endpoints and disposition

Per lineage, REF_A is success on case A under REFERENCE_M1; O_A is case A success under M0_ONLY; F_A is case A success under M0_PLUS_E1; REF_B and REF_C are B and C success under REFERENCE_M1. The complete-case reference transfer chain is REF_A AND NOT O_A AND REF_B AND REF_C. F_A is descriptive and REF_A greater than F_A is not a gate. A missing/invalid/unusable M0_ONLY A response is O_A=false for its raw endpoint but cannot count as a successful negative control; it sets O_A_CONTROL_OBSERVED=false and forces CHAIN=false for that lineage. All endpoints use fixed denominator six.

TRANSFER_INTERFACE_PARTIAL applies if not WORKING and both evaluators find usable policies at least 4/6, REF_A at least 3/6, and chain at least 3/6.
Otherwise TRANSFER_INTERFACE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.
No reconciliation or third evaluator. Preserve raw sheets and disagreements.

## Component B: revision-generation repair

Six fresh independent revision sessions, two per family. Each sees only its family M0, raw E1, the shared operational policy schema, and neutral revision prompt. No A/B/C, targets, Task226 reports, Task225 R7 responses or scores, reference policies, or sibling output may be shown. These are new sessions, not retries. They create one policy each. No transfer is run on these policies in this task.

Two fresh independent revision evaluators see family M0/E1, schema, criteria, and six generated policies, with replicate IDs opaque and family labels available only where needed to ground evidence. They score policy validity and critical errors. They do not see held-out targets, reference policies, transfer outputs, Task226 report, or the other evaluator's sheet. Both sheets lock before the fixed-denominator disposition.

REVISION_GENERATION_WORKING requires both evaluators to find at least 5/6 valid, each family represented by at least one valid policy, at least two of three families with both replicates valid, and zero unsupported global-retirement critical errors among valid policies.
REVISION_GENERATION_PARTIAL applies if not WORKING and both evaluators find at least 4/6 valid and at least two of three families represented.
Otherwise REVISION_GENERATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED. No reconciliation or third evaluator.
