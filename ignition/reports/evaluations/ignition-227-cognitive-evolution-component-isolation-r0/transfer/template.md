# Identical bundle template for every transfer session

Every one of the 18 fresh sessions receives identical byte copies of its family's cases A.md, B.md, and C.md, plus the neutral prompt and response schema. The one condition-specific method bundle is provided per the sealed coordinator manifest.

M0_ONLY method bundle: family M0 only.
M0_PLUS_E1 method bundle: family M0 and raw family E1.
REFERENCE_M1 method bundle: family M0 and the exact reviewed target-blind reference policy; raw E1 is not separately supplied.

Condition name, task lineage, targets, outcomes, evaluator criteria, thresholds, and condition map are never in the successor-visible bundle. Do not reuse a conversation or carry prior context. Do not add commentary around the JSON response.
