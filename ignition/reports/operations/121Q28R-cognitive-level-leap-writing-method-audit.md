## Conceptual root cause and correction

0.1.0 correctly described prospective writing, uncontained remainder, sudden movement, double-belonging hinges, horizontal versus vertical motion, retro-illumination, lower-level preservation, information-gain stopping and anti-fixed-stage discipline. It failed by defining the whole method through bodily experience, making the story spec's embodied anchor mandatory, requiring bodily return and downgrading work without one.

0.2.0 moves the upper method to cognitive-level motion and adds:

- a starting carrier that may be bodily, conceptual, numerical, verbal, material, mnemonic or unresolved;
- a cognitive gravity center with a manifest topic and a latent topic allowed to remain `UNNAMED`, move, fork or merge;
- recursive concept redefinition as a transition interface;
- latent-theme growth and retroactive development;
- a stop rule that also asks whether the latent topic can illuminate the main route.

Embodied anchoring is optional mode; cross-domain isomorphism is application type; high-dimensional compression is optional ending form. Double-belonging hinges remain useful but are not mandatory at every level.

## Naming provenance

The canonical method name is `之元写作法 / Zhiyuan Writing Method`. It is named for maintainer 之元 because the method was distilled from his works, cognitive movement, feedback and counterexamples, and therefore carries a personal cognitive style. `Cognitive-level leap` names the current internal generative kernel rather than the public method identity. The neutral `cognitive-level-leap-*` repository paths describe that kernel and avoid reintroducing the rejected embodied hard gate.

Naming provenance does not universalize a personal style, make it the only correct writing method, or imply that an Agent using the interface reproduces 之元. The transferable discipline remains bounded by each writer's own experience, values and judgment.

## Counterexample and source boundary

The required local and Spotlight search did not locate `每日一诗停更说明.pdf`. No PDF or full text is committed, quoted or claimed as verified. The analysis uses only the maintainer-confirmed structural summary and formal review input.

That input shows recursive redefinition of time, geography, photography and goodbye; a manifest topic of stopping publication; and a late-forming latent topic of finite life, choice and farewell. The closing farewell retroactively reorganizes the opening poem, translation time, abandoned alternatives, galactic timing and lost contact. The structure therefore defeats mandatory embodiment without proving a universal law.

`永昭·虚遐` remains a bounded example of optional embodied anchoring, prospective loading, lower-level preservation, a double-belonging hinge and compressed ending. 121Q27 remains a useful horizontal isomorphism baseline, not rewritten or published.

## Anti-template attack

- The generation loop explicitly permits return, merge, omission and reordering; it is not a five-part or nine-act surface.
- No explicit hinge is required at every level; continuity may come from recursive definition, an unresolved question, shared tension, observer change or continuous semantic/time transformation.
- Latent topics may remain unnamed and must be changed by material, not locked first and proved afterward.
- Recursive redefinition must change a concept's world or answerable question, not repeat a word per paragraph.
- More domains and greater abstraction are not quality measures.
- Bodily coordinates, concrete objects, cross-domain examples and compressed endings are not substitute hard gates.
- Template completeness, CI and author likeness cannot validate literary quality.
