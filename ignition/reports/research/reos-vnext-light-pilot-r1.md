This is a public-safe method and process result. It does not publish private relay paths, reviewer/session identifiers, local worktree paths, raw private receipts or private environment details.

## Question and frozen boundary

The pilot asked:

> In adults self-studying a fixed set of unfamiliar factual items, with total learning time and retrieval count held equal, does spaced retrieval versus one massed session increase mean proportion correct on a seven-day delayed test?

The frozen boundary was:

- adults learning non-high-stakes unfamiliar factual items;
- spaced retrieval compared with one massed session;
- total learning time and retrieval count treated as separate equality conditions;
- seven-day delayed item-level proportion correct;
- immediate recall, delayed retention, confidence, item accuracy, delay length, factual items, skills, randomized evidence and observational evidence kept distinct;
- no pooled estimate unless data and estimands were directly comparable;
- a source-scoped ceiling only, with no universal education, long-term achievement or unqualified causal claim.

## Study design

A blinded ordinary-research baseline was completed first. Only then was the same question run through `REOS_LIGHT`.

The comparison was process-level. It did not change the evidence, secretly alter the question, or treat the REOS case as a canonical claim/evidence registry.

## Results

Research result: `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_WITH_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE`

Process result: `MIXED_VALUE_WITH_COST`

The LIGHT process made several boundaries more explicit:

- source and proposition traceability;
- source-family identity and duplicate control;
- retrieval/access states and blocked routes;
- measurement-definition mismatches;
- alternative explanations and claim ceilings;
- explicit `ABSTAIN`, blocked and not-identifiable dispositions;
- independent review with repair obligations;
- reload from committed case state;
- a typed, projection-only handoff to a named receiving authority.

These additions did not make the scientific estimand identifiable. The result remains a bounded non-identification, not a failure of the question to matter and not evidence that spaced retrieval has no effect.

## What was not established

Source-change updateability and local recomputation after one source changed were not exercised.

Information gain versus bureaucracy is `UNDERDETERMINED`. The pilot observed additional objects, files, validation steps and operator work; exact active-time/retrieval-count equality, comparable delays/outcomes, full-text access and source-family independence depth remained incomplete. Waiting and failure cost were not fully quantified.

The pilot did not establish causality, universal learning effects, external validity, production readiness, recovery-layer need or any automatic Owner/epistemic acceptance.

## Architecture conclusion

The accepted kernel is useful as a bounded LIGHT process-control capability where explicit obligations, typed review, reference boundaries and committed reload matter. The evidence does not justify adding a long-running recovery runtime.

Keep:

- `ActivationDecision`;
- `ResearchCase`;
- simplified `QuestionContract`;
- `ResearchObligation` DAG;
- thin `ArtifactRefLedger`;
- minimal `EvidenceRequest`;
- noncanonical `ClaimCandidate`;
- `ReviewRequest` / `ReviewDecision`;
- projection-only `HandoffBundle`;
- deterministic validators and narrow CLI/API.

Remove from REOS ownership or keep in existing authorities:

- canonical source, claim, evidence, proof and Results Book authority;
- Charter, Epistemic Governance, Language–Thought, MCF/PSD/ARN, Function OS and Git/CI authority.

Do not implement in this round:

- `ExecutionPacket`, `ExecutorLease`, `ResumeCapsule`;
- runtime idempotency, supervisor/heartbeat or executor substitution;
- `REOS_FULL`, distributed queues, broad adapters, automatic budget optimization or UI.

Final architecture recommendation: `KEEP_LIGHT_ONLY`.

## Residuals

This pilot is one bounded research-method comparison. It does not estimate general REOS value across domains. A future recovery round may be reconsidered only after a future real task records a concrete LIGHT failure that cannot be safely handled by committed case state. Until then, no recovery/runtime expansion is authorized.
