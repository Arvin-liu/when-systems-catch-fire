# Writing Domain Pack — 之元写作法 0.5.0

This is the bounded writing and publication pack entrypoint. It routes the
declared 之元写作法 and publication validation surfaces without changing
source truth, Owner acceptance, or epistemic status.

Within Agent Platform R2 it is an L6 expression and feedback surface. It keeps
`external_input` and `ignition_increment` provenance distinct, preserves source
and framing residue, and sends public feedback back only as a candidate source or
gap. Prose quality, reader resonance, method completion or Pack validation cannot
prove facts, causality, literary quality, Owner acceptance or epistemic status.

See [`manifest.json`](./manifest.json), the
[之元写作法](../../docs/publication/zhiyuan-writing-method.md) and the
[publication showcase](../../docs/publication/zhiyuan-writing-showcase.md).
