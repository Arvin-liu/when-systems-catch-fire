# FAMILY02 — M0 turbidity-to-solids method

**Artifact ID:** CE-F02-M0-R0  
**Status:** frozen initial-method design; synthetic benchmark material only.

## Purpose and source boundary

M0 estimates suspended-solids concentration in a water sample from a nephelometric turbidity reading. Its calibration source is F02-M0-CALIBRATION, containing 36 gravimetric reference samples from mineral-dominant catchment sediment: 0–500 NTU, three sensor cells from the same instrument lot, duplicate readings, blank checks, and dry-mass measurements at constant weight. The source matrix includes sand/silt mixtures across the validated concentration range. On that original matrix, the calibration produced a bounded absolute error within 12 mg/L for 33/36 samples and correctly flagged the remaining three through replicate disagreement.

The documented M0 claim extends the same single calibration to sediment samples regardless of particle composition. Composition is not an M0 input. The method was useful on its original mineral-dominant scope; the universal extension is a stated premise rather than a validated extra condition.

## Candidate actions

- REPORT_ESTIMATE: report concentration estimate with source method and range.
- REPEAT_SAMPLE: remix, take two additional readings, and recompute the median.
- FLAG_OUT_OF_RANGE: do not extrapolate above 500 NTU or below the blank-corrected measurement limit.
- INVALID_MEASUREMENT: stop if blank drift, cell contamination, or replicate spread exceeds the method limits.

## Selection criterion and procedure

1. Confirm blank correction is between −2 and +2 NTU, inspect the cell for bubbles/contamination, and take two readings after the prescribed mixing time.
2. If duplicate difference exceeds 8 NTU, remix and take two further readings. If the new pair still differs by more than 8 NTU, emit INVALID_MEASUREMENT.
3. For a valid blank-corrected median T in 0–500 NTU, calculate estimated TSS (mg/L) = 1.35 × T + 12.
4. If T is outside the validated range, emit FLAG_OUT_OF_RANGE. Otherwise emit REPORT_ESTIMATE with the calculated estimate and the calibration uncertainty band of ±12 mg/L for the original calibration matrix.

The discriminating observations are the paired turbidity readings, blank and cell checks, and the independent constant-weight gravimetric reference in the calibration source. A repeated stable reading distinguishes sample measurement noise from calibration error.

## Expected outcomes and failure interpretation

On the original mineral-dominant samples, expected estimates track the gravimetric references inside the recorded uncertainty band. For example, F02-M0-CALIBRATION#CAL-05 has T=100 NTU, estimate 147 mg/L, and gravimetric 151 mg/L; #CAL-19 has T=300 NTU, estimate 417 mg/L, and gravimetric 409 mg/L. #CAL-34 has T=498 NTU, valid readings, and a gravimetric result 12 mg/L outside the band; its recorded replicate-spread rule routes it to repeat review rather than silently certifying a point estimate.

An invalid blank or persistent replicate spread is a measurement failure and is reported as such; a valid sample beyond the range is not extrapolated. A valid in-range estimate outside the uncertainty band is retained as a calibration residual for method review, not removed from the record.

## Disposition

Use this artifact as the unchanged M0 baseline for the later revision task. Its single calibration, formula, input set, and original validated mineral-dominant behavior remain the comparison state. No revised relationship is predeclared here.
