# FAMILY01 — M0 canopy-temperature triage method

**Artifact ID:** CE-F01-M0-R0  
**Status:** frozen initial-method design; synthetic benchmark material only.

## Purpose and source boundary

This method triages field blocks for an independent soil-moisture confirmation. It does not itself authorize irrigation or establish plant stress as an external fact. Its synthetic calibration basis is F01-M0-VALIDATION, a set of paired canopy scans, contact-leaf temperature checks, soil probes, and plant-water observations on ordinary mineral soil and dark organic mulch.

The calibration set covers calibrated radiometric cameras, a matched shielded air sensor, canopy height 0.4–1.2 m, ambient temperature 12–32 °C, solar-noon scans, and view angles within 25° of nadir. Across 32 blocks spanning both listed ground surfaces, the calibrated thermal contrast separated the prespecified soil-moisture-confirmed review cases from routine cases. The method was useful in its validated scope. Its declared limitation is that it does not encode ground-surface reflectance or a reflection check; its operating premise is that ground radiance does not materially contaminate the canopy statistic.

## Candidates and actions

- ROUTINE_MONITORING: no additional thermal-triggered check.
- SOIL_MOISTURE_CONFIRMATION: send a technician to measure volumetric water content (VWC) at the fixed root-zone probe.
- REPEAT_THERMAL_SCAN: collect a second scan after checking camera and air-sensor setup.
- INPUT_INVALID: stop when required calibration, time, geometry, or sensor-quality fields are absent.

Thermal output only prioritizes confirmation. A separate site agronomist applies the site's irrigation rule to the soil probe and plant-water evidence.

## Selection procedure

1. Confirm the radiometric camera's blackbody check is within ±0.2 °C; verify the paired shielded air sensor, camera time, plot, scan geometry, and canopy mask.
2. For each scan, calculate D = median masked canopy temperature − matched air temperature.
3. Take two scans ten minutes apart from the same marked block. If setup or mask quality fails, use INPUT_INVALID. If the pair differs by more than 0.8 °C, use REPEAT_THERMAL_SCAN.
4. If both D values are at least 2.0 °C, select SOIL_MOISTURE_CONFIRMATION. If both are below 2.0 °C, select ROUTINE_MONITORING. If the pair straddles 2.0 °C, select REPEAT_THERMAL_SCAN.
5. Under the independent site protocol, VWC below 16% triggers an agronomist review; VWC at or above 16% leaves the block in routine monitoring unless separate plant-water evidence warrants review. Do not infer irrigation from D alone.

## Discriminating observations and expected outcomes

The method expects repeatable D at or above 2.0 °C to prioritize a VWC check, while lower repeatable values stay in routine monitoring. The fixed VWC observation discriminates whether the thermal trigger coincides with low root-zone water; the paired-scan difference discriminates a stable thermal signal from setup noise.

F01-M0-VALIDATION includes source locators VAL-01 through VAL-32. Examples: VAL-04 has D=2.4/2.5 °C, VWC 12.8%, and corroborating plant-water observations; VAL-17 has D=0.8/0.9 °C, VWC 23.1%, and no stress indication; VAL-29 repeats the high-trigger/low-VWC result on dark organic mulch. The fixed procedure correctly routes these examples to confirmation or routine monitoring, respectively.

## Failure interpretation and disposition

- Failed setup or missing required fields: INPUT_INVALID; reacquire the missing measurement.
- Pair disagreement above the stated limit or a threshold-straddling pair: REPEAT_THERMAL_SCAN.
- A repeated threshold trigger without low VWC: record the false-positive triage and do not irrigate on thermal evidence alone.
- Low VWC with no thermal trigger: retain the independent site review pathway; a low thermal value does not veto other evidence.

M0 applies its same D rule to any ground surface in its declared field scope because its procedure has no surface-reflectance input. It retains the stated scope and source basis here as the comparison baseline; this artifact does not predeclare a revision.
