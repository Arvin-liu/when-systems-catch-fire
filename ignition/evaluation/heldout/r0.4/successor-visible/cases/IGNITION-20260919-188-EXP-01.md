# IGNITION-20260919-188-EXP-01

## Synthetic boundary

This is a bounded synthetic experience-research case. The setting, measurements, and participants are simulated; no real person or private record is used.

## Context

A public-service kiosk pilot was observed across three weekday sessions. The kiosk offered two navigation labels for the same service: `start a request` and `begin service`. A neutral observer recorded aggregate interaction events but did not interview visitors and did not infer intent from unrecorded behavior.

The visible event stream contains:

- 24 completed sessions and 7 abandoned sessions;
- 11 completed sessions that displayed both navigation labels before completion;
- 5 abandoned sessions that displayed both labels;
- a median pause of 18 seconds between the first label display and the next recorded event;
- no event recording the visitor's reason for pausing or abandoning;
- one maintenance interval during which label-display events were not collected.

The two labels were shown in different positions in the interface. The event stream does not establish whether position, wording, device familiarity, or another factor affected any session. The aggregate counts also do not identify individual sessions beyond the event categories stated above.

## Response surface

Account for the case by separating direct observations from interpretations. Mark what remains unknown, and identify evidence that would reduce the uncertainty. Keep the missing maintenance-interval events and the absence of visitor explanations visible in the account.
