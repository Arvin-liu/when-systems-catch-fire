# IGNITION-20260919-188-GOV-01

## Synthetic boundary

This is a bounded synthetic engineering-governance case about an audit-log schema transition. All repository names, records, and timestamps are fabricated for protocol testing.

## Context

A service moved from `audit-ledger-v1` to `audit-ledger-v2`. The migration package contains a field map, a dry-run report, a rollback note, and a post-deploy status snapshot.

The available artifacts state:

- `actor_ref` and `event_kind` are required in both versions;
- `context_blob` is optional in v1 and required in v2 only for `event_kind=configuration`;
- the dry run processed 412 synthetic records and reported 3 records needing manual review;
- the rollback note says the deployment can be reverted, but does not record whether the three records were restored;
- the status snapshot reports `migration_complete=true` and `review_queue=3`;
- the field map has no entry for the ordering behavior of events written during the deployment window.

The artifacts identify no person, credential, production customer, or private operational data. They do not establish why any record entered the review queue, whether the rollback path was exercised, or whether deployment-window ordering is preserved.

## Response surface

Separate the facts present in the artifacts from interpretations about migration correctness. Mark unknowns and specify evidence that would be needed to resolve them. Preserve the distinction between a status flag, a migration record, and an independently verified audit trail.
