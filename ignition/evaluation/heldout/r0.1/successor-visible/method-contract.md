# Observable Method Contract R0.1

For each bounded source, produce an inspectable record that:

1. identifies the source and binds every material statement to a source locator;
2. distinguishes direct observations, source-reported claims, interpretation,
   hypotheses, and unknowns;
3. represents objects and directed relations without silently completing gaps;
4. records provenance, uncertainty, boundaries, and unresolved obligations;
5. gives a claim ceiling and forbidden inferences;
6. proposes one bounded continuation with prerequisites and a stop condition.

Use only observable decisions and evidence in the record. Do not write hidden
chain-of-thought. Do not infer external truth, novelty, causality, capability,
generalization, cognitive inheritance, or acceptance from the source or from
successful serialization. Preserve ambiguity where the source does not settle
it.
