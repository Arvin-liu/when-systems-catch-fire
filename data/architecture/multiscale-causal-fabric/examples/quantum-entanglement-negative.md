# Quantum Entanglement Misprojection Negative Example

Machine file: `quantum-entanglement-negative.json`

This negative example records the boundary that quantum entanglement can support nonclassical correlation, but must not be used as a controllable superluminal communication channel or as a general explanation for social, industrial, interpersonal, or AI systems.

The important result is that cross-scale borrowing of a term is not evidence transfer. In non-quantum contexts, "entanglement" must remain `analogy_only` unless real mediators and evidence are supplied.

Boundary: this is not a technical proof in quantum information theory. It is a repository safety example for preventing metaphor from becoming mechanism.
