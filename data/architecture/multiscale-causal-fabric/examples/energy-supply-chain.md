# Energy Supply Chain Fabric

Machine file: `energy-supply-chain.json`

This example uses energy technology supply chains to show long-range influence without action at a distance. Extraction, processing, manufacturing, shipping, finance, standards, policy, grid integration, and maintenance form a mediated chain with delay, bottlenecks, attenuation, and amplification.

The important result is that "global" causal range does not imply a superluminal or unmediated relation. It means many local steps are linked across scales.

Boundary: this example does not identify the exact supplier chain for any specific project. Those missing local mediators remain residue.

Key source used as provenance: IEA, `Securing Clean Energy Technology Supply Chains`.
